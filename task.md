Task: Build a web product that closes a fundamendal pain for adults in the US
Audience: US, 25-45 age (working adults with jobs, bills, families, and goals)
Model: B2C
Platform: Web only, no mobile apps

I need to pick one track only:
1. Money: spending, earning, saving, owing. Where does money hurt?
2. Entertainment: play, stories, free time. What makes downtime feel wasted?
3. Skills: Learning and growing. What do people fail to master?

Track is a territory, not product. We must narrow it down to one user and one pain.

Judgement:
40%: a real working product, high quality UX and code.
20%: clear what the user pays for and why he comes back tomorrow
15%: a clear choice of problem and a deep understanding of the target user
15%: business potential. is it a big or growing niche, $100M ARR, or LTV > CAC and so on
10%: originality. not a copy paste of the business that already exists
